# Contributing to this project

First of all, thank you for checking out my project! 

Since this is an experimental and learning-focused repository, I'm mainly using it to practice my design and coding skills. However, I truly appreciate any kind of feedback.

## How to contribute?
If you'd like to help or share your thoughts:

1. **Open an Issue:** If you find a bug or have a suggestion, feel free to open an issue. Let's discuss it!
2. **Share Feedback:** Since I'm focusing on design and UI, your feedback on the project's layout or aesthetics is very valuable to me.
3. **Be Kind:** This is a place for learning and growth. Please keep all feedback constructive and respectful.

I'm always looking to improve, so feel free to reach out!
